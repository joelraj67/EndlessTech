# Design System Specification: Aura Enterprise (EndlessTech)

Generated via Stitch MCP for EndlessTech Enterprise Consultancy & Academy.

## 1. Overview & Brand Style: "Dark Editorial & Prestige-Tech"
A hybrid of Minimalism and Glassmorphism for high-end technology consultancy and IT academy:
- Canvas: Deep Jet Black (#16262E) editorial background.
- Typography: Manrope ExtraBold/Bold display headings with tight tracking (-0.02em) and Inter/Manrope body.
- Glassmorphism: FrostedSmoked Charcoal Blue (#2E4756) containers with 16px-20px backdrop blurs and 1px Thistle (#DBC2CF) translucent borders.
- Signal Accents: Cerulean (#3C7A89) for primary buttons/nav, Terracotta (#B0552E) for high-impact innovation CTAs and prompt glyphs.

## 2. Color Palette & Semantics
- **Jet Black (`#16262E`):** Page canvas background & dark panels.
- **Charcoal Blue (`#2E4756`):** Secondary container surface, frosted card fills (20-40% opacity).
- **Cerulean (`#3C7A89`):** Primary action color, link hovers, active focus rings.
- **Thistle (`#DBC2CF`):** Light-neutral typography, soft badges, glass stroke highlights.
- **Terracotta (`#B0552E`):** High-impact CTA buttons, CLI prompt glyphs (`>`), active indicators.

## 3. Elevation & Glassmorphic Surface Specs
- `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` to `blur(20px)`
- Borders: `1px solid rgba(219, 194, 207, 0.12)` (Thistle translucent stroke)
- Card Padding: `36px - 40px` (`p-9` to `p-10`)
- Section Vertical Rhythm: `120px - 140px` (`py-36`)
